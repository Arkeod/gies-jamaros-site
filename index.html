Frequently asked

FAQ

What is Gies?

Gies (Games Industry Executive Summary) is a weekly intelligence newsletter covering the full video games market through five lenses: players & audience, markets, products, supply, and business models. Written for executives, investors, and senior analysts. Published every Friday since June 2024.

Who writes Gies?

Emmanuel Rosier (founder, prepares the weekly newsletter), Casey Al-Kaisy (co-founder, hosts the podcast), and David Thier (writes the monthly takeaway with guests). Patrick Rose and Andre Persson are recurring podcast guests.

Where can I read Gies?

Gies publishes weekly on Substack at giesweekly.substack.com. Long-form video analyses are on YouTube at @giesweekly. Brand updates run on the Gies LinkedIn company page.

Who is the audience?

People in the gaming industry who want a fast factual read of the week. Senior operators, investors, equity analysts, and consultants.

How is Gies different from other gaming newsletters?

Gies is for industry insiders, not consumer gaming readers. The newsletter is purely factual: curated news with no editorial spin. Opinion lives in the monthly takeaway and the weekly podcast.

What does the paid tier unlock?

$5/month unlocks the Monthly Takeaway: an opinionated longer read by David Thier and a guest on the most consequential gaming story of the month.
