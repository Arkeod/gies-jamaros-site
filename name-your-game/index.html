Name your game.

The most common words in video game titles, what is rising and fading, and how crowded your own title words are.

Steam shipped 21,170 games in 2025. Your title is the first thing players see and the cheapest discoverability decision you will make: the words in it decide whether you surface in search and whether you disappear into a pile of lookalikes. We analyzed 128,363 Steam releases to map which words are crowded, which are rising, and where the open space is. The most crowded word: simulator, in 2,398 titles, and as of 2026 the most used word in Steam history, having finally overtaken VR, which collapsed from 7.2% of new releases in 2017 to 0.6% in 2025.

No word appears in more than 2% of titles. Steam's catalog is so fragmented that even the most common naming choice is rare, which is exactly why developers lean on searchable words: in a store shipping 21,170 games a year (2025), the title is the cheapest discoverability lever there is.

The biggest shifts since 2020

Comparing each word's share of new releases in 2020-21 against 2024-25:

Change in share of new releases, 2020-21 vs 2024-25 (percentage points)
Falling ←→ Rising

Every major faller is either the VR bet unwinding or the pandemic casual wave receding. Jigsaw went from 0.84% of 2021 releases to 0.10% in 2025; solitaire followed the same curve. Every major riser maps to a proven commercial template: survivors (3 titles in 2021, 68 in 2023, the Vampire Survivors effect), backrooms (1 title in 2021, 66 in 2025), clicker and idle (the monetization-friendly incremental genre), and cats, which went from 0.12% to 0.81% of releases, a 7x increase driven largely by the hidden-object-with-cats template.

Four trajectories, 2015 to 2025

Share of each year's releases containing the word

Two different declines are visible. VR fell for eight straight years from its 7.2% peak: a platform bet unwinding slowly. Puzzle spiked to 1.6% in 2021 with the pandemic casual wave, then halved by 2025: a demand cycle. Simulator is the only word that has compounded through both cycles, reaching 2.5% of all 2025 releases, the highest share any word has posted since VR in 2019.

How to stand out

The SEO trap. Title-as-SEO is now the dominant naming strategy on Steam: the words gaining share (simulator, hidden, idle, clicker) are the ones players type into the search bar. But a searchable word puts you in the results next to every other game that made the same calculation. Simulator gets you found and buried in the same move: 530 rivals in 2025 alone.

Read the trend, not just the count. A word riding a proven template (survivors went from 3 titles in 2021 to 68 in 2023, backrooms from 1 to 66) signals a wave that will be saturated by the time you ship. A word fading (jigsaw, solitaire, VR) signals demand that already moved on. The chart above tells you which curve you are joining.

The caveat. This measures what gets published, not what sells. A crowded word means crowded supply, not proven demand; an empty word means open space, not an audience. Use the count to size the competition, then validate the demand side separately.
